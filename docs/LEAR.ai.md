## Translation method

- Each Toki Pona word is replaced by an აი sign from the 155-sign core (dictionary: docs/dictionary.md).
- Particles li/e/la are kept as words (signs ა/ე/თ) — Toki Pona grammar in sign script.
- Proper names are diphthongs (one-symbol signs): Lear → Ḭ (per /lɪə/).
- Minimalism inevitably compresses: this is a reduction of the play's spirit, not a literal translation (Toki Pona translation tradition).
