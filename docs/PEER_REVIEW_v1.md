## 2. General assessment

The manuscript describes აი, the logical limit of minimalism in the spirit of Toki Pona. The concept is interesting and can inform discussion on the limits of phonological inventory reduction and orthographic load. **However, in its current form it is a design document, not a scientific article:** no (a) theory of root selection, (b) formal phonology, (c) any empirical validation, (d) verifiable claims, (e) half of the references unverified. Recommendation: **Major Revision**.

## 3. Strengths

1. **Clear design concept.** "One phoneme per word" is a clear, testable constraint.
2. **Typological positioning.** Correct reference to minimal inventories (Pirahã 11, Rotokas 11, Hawaiian 13, Toki Pona 14).
3. **Orthographic integration.** Georgian script with historical letters (ჲ ჶ ჸ) — rare and valuable.

## 4. Major comments

### M1. Root selection lacks theoretical grounding (SEVERE)
"Subjective selection" admitted in text. **Requirement:** ground in Natural Semantic Metalanguage (Wierzbicka, 1996; Goddard, 2018) — 65 NSM primes; show which of the 14 map onto primes (EXIST, I, YOU, GOOD, BAD, NOT, PERSON, THING, TIME, PLACE, KNOW, MANY — 12 of 14) and justify extensions (speech, deixis).

### M2. Zero empirical validation (SEVERE)
No experiments: (a) learnability (cf. ROILA, where Toki Pona vocabulary improved speech recognition), (b) communication efficiency, (c) cross-cultural transparency of roots. **Requirement:** pilot study or explicit limitation (see M9).

### M3. Phonology not formalized (SEVERE)
No feature matrix, no phonotactic constraints (Prince & Smolensky, 2004), no explanation of bans (*wu, *wo, *ji, *ti), no comparison with typological patterns (Maddieson, 2013, WALS ch. 1).

### M4. Expressivity not demonstrated (SEVERE)
"The whole world in 14 signs" is rhetoric. Need combinatorial analysis (14¹ + 14² + 14³), comparison with Toki Pona's 120-word coverage, ambiguity estimate.

### M5. Numerals by diphthongs — ad hoc (MEDIUM)
272 one-symbol numerals without positional system. **Paradox:** the zero-sign 0 exists but is not used positionally.

### M6. Reference verification — unverifiable items (SEVERE)
See Section 6: [1] (Blahuš) and [4] (Firchow & Firchow) not found in Crossref; [7] (Parker Jones) wrong DOI and year (correct: 2017, 10.1017/S0025100316000438).

### M7. No Limitations/Ethics sections (MEDIUM)
Missing discussion of cultural aspects of Georgian script, absence of speaker community, Sapir–Whorf relevance (Whorf, 1956).

### M8. No comparison with the broader class (MEDIUM)
Beyond Toki Pona: Lojban, Basic English, Interlingua, pidgins; no meta-analysis of why previous minimalisms worked/failed.

### M9. Scientific contribution unclear (MEDIUM)
What does science learn? Options: (a) lower bound of phoneme reduction, (b) role of orthography, (c) practical minimal semantic primitives.

## 7. Meta-analysis

**Root problem:** genre — the manuscript describes a construction, not an investigation. Three systemic vulnerabilities: (1) no counterfactual benchmark (why these 14 roots and not any others); (2) circular minimalism logic (14 phonemes ↔ 14 roots); (3) orthographic vs phonological load conflated.
